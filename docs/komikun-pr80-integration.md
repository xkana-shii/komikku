## Merge migration semantics

The `merge` / `merged_entry` split in `63.sqm` is byte-identical to Komi-kun's final `61.sqm`: references
whose child manga is gone are dropped, children are kept regardless of whether their source extension is
installed (the split only looks at manga ids), the self-reference's sort mode and the info entry move to
`merge`, and a duplicated (merge, manga) pair keeps its oldest row. The old-fork converter runs the same
statements.
